The car accelerates by itself (turn Auto-accelerate off to use the GAS pedal). BRAKE slows you down; hold it when stopped to reverse. NITRO gives a boost, 📣 honks. Steer with buttons, the wheel or by tilting your phone. In Cockpit view, drag the steering wheel. Tap CAM any time to switch views. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space nitro, H horn, C camera.

The road is two-way. Overtake in the oncoming lane when it is clear. Crash and the car stops for about three seconds, then you are set back on the road. Coins are won by finishing high and finishing fast, not by driving.
